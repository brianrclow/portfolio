Boise State Student Org App

Developed a mobile app for a student organization, serving as a central hub for all their links and information, available on both iOS and Android. The app included features for calendar events, photo albums, push notifications, and social links. Led a student team in the design and development process, overseeing everything from the app icon and logo to the organization and layout of information.

*Removed from app stores in 2023. A redesigned successor now serves the same purpose.

Tech Stack

TypeScript, Angular, Ionic, Firebase

Soccer Fan App

In Progress

A native mobile app concept for supporters of a local soccer club, built around three principles: fast (quick to open, usable even on poor stadium wifi), informative (clear league, team, schedule, and game info without the clutter), and built for one team rather than a generic multi-league experience. Planned features include live matchday notifications, a digital matchday program, season schedule syncing, and fan-specific extras like stadium info and supporter content — with an admin tool planned so content can be updated without shipping a new app release.

Tech Stack

Swift (iOS), Kotlin (Android), NestJS, PostgreSQL
